SARO NABI VIEW — app information

SARO NABI VIEW · macOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 Nov 2026
This policy covers the macOS app.

SARO NABI VIEW is a free, open-source app for viewing and organizing images on Mac. You can use it without creating an account.

On-device processing and transmission

SARO NABI VIEW processes the images, archives, and embedded metadata you open locally on your Mac. The app does not upload these files or information to a developer server. It does not include features that collect or transmit data to third parties for advertising, usage analytics, or user tracking.

Information stored on your Mac

The app may store preferences and shortcuts, window state, recently opened folders and archives, saved pages, bookmarks, recent file-move destinations, and records of file access you granted. Thumbnails, display caches, and temporary files extracted for viewing archives may also be stored on your device. This information supports viewing, reopening files, and remembering your preferences.

Retention and deletion

You can turn off recent-item history in settings and remove recent items and bookmarks in the app. You can clear the disk cache in settings. The general data-clearing action may leave your preferences and bookmarks in place. To remove remaining app data completely, quit the app and use your operating system’s app-data or cache controls, or file-management tools. Caches and temporary files are removed according to the app’s cleanup rules; some temporary files may remain after an unexpected shutdown.

Simply viewing an image does not change the contents of the original file. File-move and Trash actions that you explicitly choose change the location of the affected files.

External services

If you visit the app’s information or support website, or use an external service, that service’s privacy policy applies. Information handled by your operating system or the App Store is governed by those services’ settings and policies. This policy describes the SARO NABI VIEW macOS app itself.
