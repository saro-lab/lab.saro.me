NABI Player — app information

NABI Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 Nov 2026
This policy applies to NABI Player for Mac and NABI Player Mobile for iPhone and iPad.

NABI Player plays video files you choose on your device. You can use it without creating an account.

On-device processing and transmission

The app reads and plays the videos and subtitles you select on your device. It does not send videos, subtitles, or viewing history to the developer, and does not use them for advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app stores recent file entries, playback positions, bookmarks, and playback preferences locally on your device. On iPhone and iPad it also stores access records so it can reopen files you selected. This information is used to resume playback, reopen files, and remember your preferences.

Retention and deletion

You can remove recent entries and bookmarks from the library in the app. Removing an entry does not delete the original video file. To remove all remaining local app data, use your operating system’s app-data removal controls, which may require uninstalling the app.

External services

When you select a document from a cloud provider in the Files app, that provider may retrieve the file for your device under its own privacy policy. Information handled by your operating system, app store, or cloud file provider is governed by those services’ settings and policies.
